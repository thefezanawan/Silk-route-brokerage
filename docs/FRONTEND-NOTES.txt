SILK ROUTE BROKERAGE LLC — REDESIGNED WEBSITE
=============================================

FILES
  index.html                   The complete site. Self-contained: all images are embedded, no assets folder needed.
  silk-route-standalone.html   Identical copy of index.html (same file, different name).
  Use either one. Just upload ONE file to your host (rename it index.html if needed).

HOW TO PREVIEW (exact steps)
  1. Right-click silk-route-redesign.zip > Extract All (do NOT open files from inside the zip preview).
  2. Open the extracted folder and double-click index.html.

WHERE TO EDIT (top of the <script> block in either HTML file)
  ENDPOINT              form-handling URL. Empty = quote opens the visitor's email app (kwilliams@silkroutebrokerage.com).
  NOTIFICATIONS         add {title:"...",date:"...",text:"..."}
  RATING                4.8 (the overall rating shown in Reviews)
  REVIEWS               add REAL reviews: {name:"...",role:"...",text:"..."}
  SHOW_SAMPLE_REVIEWS   true = labelled "Sample · Placeholder" cards show while REVIEWS is empty. Set false to hide them.

EMAIL
  Every email link and the quote form now open a small window with: Open in Gmail, Open in Outlook,
  Open my email app, and Copy. So it works even on computers with no default mail app.

ADDING REAL REVIEWS (replaces the 4 sample cards automatically)
  const REVIEWS=[
    {name:"Robert Chen",  text:"The exact words Robert approved."},
    {name:"Sarah Williams", text:"..."},
  ];

BEFORE GOING LIVE
  - Replace the 3 sample testimonial cards with real reviews (or set SHOW_SAMPLE_REVIEWS=false).
  - Confirm the 4.8/5 rating and the "27 years combined" claim are accurate and can be published.
  - Blog thumbnails load from static.wixstatic.com (as in the original). If a thumbnail fails, a fallback photo shows.
  - Add a social preview image (og:image) with a full URL once you know your domain.
  - The aerial dock photo (iStock, watermarked) and Truck.jpg (UG Transportation reference) were NOT used.
